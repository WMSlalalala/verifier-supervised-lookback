\section{Related work}
\paragraph{Memory for GUI agents.} AutoDroid~\citep{autodroid}, AppAgent~\citep{appagent}, Agent Workflow Memory~\citep{awm}, Synapse~\citep{synapse}, and the tips and shortcuts of Mobile-Agent-E~\citep{mobileagente} store knowledge that transfers across episodes. Mobile-Agent-E's note-taker and GUI-Rise's rolling summary~\citep{guirise} write notes at observation time, before the agent knows the need. PAL-UI~\citep{palui} trains a vision agent to look back at chosen frames on 8.6K curated step-level instructions. Our verifier replaces that supervision: its labels come from a substring test over OCR text, so the training set grows with any corpus of trajectories that records typed text.

\paragraph{Gist-and-lookup and learned retrieval.} ReadAgent~\citep{readagent}, MemGPT~\citep{memgpt}, and Generative Agents~\citep{generativeagents} retrieve from short per-item lines or scored streams; document expansion~\citep{doc2query} trains the writer of those lines from downstream success, and tool self-supervision~\citep{toolformer} filters tool calls by whether they reduce later loss. We train the retriever instead of the writer, after finding that the writer's ceiling is the screen itself (\S\ref{sec:index}), with an exact substring check in place of a loss proxy.

\paragraph{Long-context evaluation.} Lost-in-the-middle~\citep{lostmiddle} and RULER~\citep{ruler} show that models use long contexts unevenly; ours is a needle task on real screens, and the filters of \S\ref{sec:index} are specific to needles mined from trajectories.
